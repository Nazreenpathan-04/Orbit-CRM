Core capabilities

One workspace for the customer journey.

The proposed first release focuses on practical tools that help a small team keep customer information accurate and sales work visible.

◎

Contact management

Keep customer names, contact details, company information, and notes together in searchable records.

⇧

Spreadsheet import

Import customer records from CSV or Excel, validate required fields, and flag possible duplicates.

↗

Lead pipeline

Track leads through clear stages, from new enquiry to qualified opportunity and closed outcome.

♧

Team assignment

Give each lead an owner so employees know which customers they are responsible for.

✓

Tasks and follow-ups

Record calls, meetings, reminders, and next steps so important conversations do not get missed.

▥

Performance dashboard

See key counts such as total contacts, open leads, pipeline stages, and completed activities.

Simple workflow

From new record to next action.

A straightforward workflow keeps responsibilities clear for administrators and employees.

01 / ADMINAdd or import customer records
02 / ADMINCheck data and assign leads
03 / EMPLOYEEContact customers and log activity
04 / EMPLOYEEUpdate lead stage and follow-up
05 / ADMINReview progress on dashboard
